## Koori's Adventure 

Blurb about the game 
Koori's Adventure is a 2D RPG where you play as a cat exploring and saving the world. 
As Koori the cat, you'll adventure into the unknown, save the villagers from magical mishpas caused by the rogue Cat witch, or settle down and build a peaceful life through farming. 
The choice is yours. 

[Code can be found here](https://github.com/limesoda314/Koori-Adventure-Game)

## Current progress: 

### Player input & Core Mechanics 
* Move using AWSD or arrow keys
* Press X to interact with objects or talk with NPCs 
* Collect items or take damage by running through interactable world objects or enemies 
* Press E to open inventory 
* Use mouse scrollbar or numbers keys (1-9) to switch to hotbar item
* Press C to use the selected hotbar item (consume item or use weapon)
* Attack enemies using equipped weapons in hotbar 
* Hover over hotbar or inventory items to view descriptions 
* Right-click to split item stacks and drag them to new slots 
* Left-click to:
    * Move items between slots 
    * Swap items 
    * Combine stacks of the same item
